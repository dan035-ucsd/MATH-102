\section{Error}
\subsection{Approximation}
\noindent Let x be an exact number. \\
Let y be an approximation of x. \\ \\
\textbf{Def:} The absolute error is $|x-y|$. \\
\textbf{Def:} The relative error is $\displaystyle \frac{|x-y|}{|x|}$, 


\subsection{Round-off Error}
Usually a machine uses a finite amount of memory to store a number.

$\text{machine} \leftarrow \pi$
\\
Suppose a machine can store some number of digits. 
\begin{enumerate}[1.]
    \item Allocate a digit for sign, positive or negative.
    \item Allocate a number of digits for the mantissa.
    \item Allocate a digit for sign of the exponent.
    \item Allocate a number of digits for the exponent.
\end{enumerate}

\noindent Under rounding in the case of a 4 digit mantissa, $\pi = 0.3142 \cdot 10^1$, this is the machine $\#$ representation of pi. \\

\noindent \textbf{Def:} Round-off error are all errors arising from the error between actual $\#$ and machine $\#$. \\

\noindent \textbf{Notation:} 
\begin{align*}
x &= \text{real } \# \\
fl(x) &= \text{machine } \# \text{ representation of } x
\end{align*}

\noindent In an s-digit rounding machine,
    
Abs error = $|x=fl(x)|$

Rel error = $\displaystyle \frac{|x-fl(x)|}{|x|}$
    $\le \frac{1}{2} \cdot 10^{1-5} = 5\cdot 10^{-5}$

\subsection{Floating Point Arithmetic}
What happens under arithmetic operations? (+, -, $\cdot$, /) \\

\noindent Two things to watch out for:
\begin{enumerate}[1.]
    \item Many operations can degrade error.
    \item Catastrophic cancellation.
\end{enumerate}

\subsubsection{Degrading Error}
\[\begin{matrix}
    \text{number} & \text{machine} \\
    x & fl(x) \\
    y & fl(y) \\
    x+y & fl(fl(x)+fl(y))
\end{matrix}\]

The more nested $fl()$ there are, the larger the error will be. The same follows for -, $\cdot$, /. \\

When performing arithmetic, it temporarily allows for more digits than the mantissa allows. \\ 

\noindent Algorithmic "solutions"

$((x+y)+z)+w$

Worst case, 4 times the error.

$(x+y)+(z+w)$

Worst case, 3 times the error. 


\subsubsection{Catastrophic Cancellation}
\textbf{Ex: } 3-digit rounding machine. Given 2 numbers: 0.312 and 0.311.
\begin{gather*} 0.312 - 0.311 = 0.001 = 0.1 \cdot 10^{-2} \\ \text{Loss of significant digits!} 
\end{gather*}

\noindent \textbf{Ex: } 5-digit rounding machine. Given 2 numbers:
\begin{align*}
x &= 0.41626324 \\
y &= 0.41626323 \\
x-y &= 0.00000001 \\
\text{In machine:} \\
fl(x) &= 0.41626 \\
fl(y) &= 0.41626 \\
fl(x)-fl(y) &= 0.00000 = 0 \\
fl(fl(x)-fl(y)) &= 0 \\ 
\text{Relative error:} \\
\frac{|x-y-fl(fl(x)-fl(y))|}{|x-y|} &= \frac{|x-y-0|}{|x-y|}\\
&= 1 = 100\% \text{ error!}
\end{align*}
